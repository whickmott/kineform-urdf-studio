Viewport notice

Desktop studio recommended

KineForm URDF Studio is designed as a desktop development workspace. On a phone or similarly small touch display, viewing is supported but editing, panel management and precise robot work will be significantly less usable.

For building or modifying a URDF, use a larger desktop or laptop display.
