KineForm changes:
- Kept the tutorial's R2D2-like structure as a compact example.
- Retained the official DAE finger and fingertip mesh references.
- Included representative continuous, revolute, prismatic and fixed joints,
  collision geometry, inertial data and joint dynamics.

The DAE meshes and their TIFF textures are loaded from the same upstream
repository through jsDelivr.
