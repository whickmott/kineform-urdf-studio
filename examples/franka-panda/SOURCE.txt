KineForm changes:
- Flattened the Panda description into plain URDF for direct browser loading.
- Uses the upstream detailed DAE files for visual geometry.
- Uses the upstream simplified STL files for collision geometry.
- Leaves visual materials unset in the URDF so the DAE files keep their native
  embedded material colours.

The Panda DAE assets carry their appearance in the Collada material data; they
do not require separate image files for the standard model.
